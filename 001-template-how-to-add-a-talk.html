Welcome

A warm greeting, and a sentence saying what we are about to hear.

The reading

Read the passage slowly and clearly.

The talk

One clear point, said plainly, three times over. Short sentences. No jargon.

A picture or a story people can hold onto.

What this means for us today, and what it means when we come to die.

Prayer

A short prayer, spoken slowly, that anyone can pray along with.

Hymn or song

Something familiar and singable.

Notes for the speaker

Keep to about ten minutes. Speak up, face the room, and pause often.
